Our Journey

Notable milestones.

From a signed partnership to a movement of holistic transformation — a chronicle of God's faithfulness through every season of our work on Mfangano Island.

The Beginning

Where our story began.

On the shores of Lake Victoria, a vision took root — to release children from poverty in Jesus' name through holistic development.

July 1, 2017
Founding Moment

Partnership Agreement Signed

A strategic partnership was formally signed between Sena PEFA Church and Compassion International Kenya, laying the foundation for holistic child development on Mfangano Island — a covenant that would change generations.

1 Strategic Covenant
Church × Compassion International

August 15, 2017
Launch Day

Program Officially Launched

KE0274 PEFA Mfangano CYDC was officially commissioned at Sena Centre, PEFA Cathedral Sena in Suba West Sub-County — marking the beginning of a new chapter for hundreds of families across Mfangano Island and the surrounding communities.

Homa Bay County

Integrated Programs Expansion

Launched integrated programs in agriculture, entrepreneurship, WASH (Water, Sanitation & Hygiene), and environmental conservation — equipping families with practical skills to break cycles of poverty and build sustainable livelihoods.
