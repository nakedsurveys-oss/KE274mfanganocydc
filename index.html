Who We Are

A foundation built on faith, love, and unwavering commitment.

We are committed to the holistic development of children and youth, aiming to free them from spiritual, physical, socio-emotional, and economic poverty in Jesus' name — so they can become self-reliant, Christ-centered adults who transform their communities.

Our Mission

To holistically release needy children and youth from poverty in Jesus' name. We are dedicated to nurturing every aspect of a child's growth — spiritually, physically, socially, emotionally, and economically — from pregnancy through young adulthood.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every child grows into a fulfilled Christian adult who knows Christ, makes Him known, and is self-reliant. Our ultimate goal is to empower children and youth to reach their full potential and become self-sustained and fulfilled members of their communities.

Our Story

Rooted in faith. Transforming lives since 2017.

What began as a partnership between Sena PEFA Church and Compassion International Kenya has grown into a movement of holistic transformation across Mfangano Island and the surrounding communities of Suba Central.

Sena Centre, PEFA Cathedral Sena · Suba Central
Location & Partnership

A Strategic Alliance

Based at Sena Centre, PEFA Cathedral Sena in Suba Central Sub-County, Homa Bay County — a strategic partnership between Sena PEFA Church and Compassion International Kenya serving the island communities of Lake Victoria.

July — August 2017

Partnership Signed, Program Launched

The partnership agreement was signed on July 1, 2017, and the program officially launched on August 15, 2017. The first cohort of participants was enrolled in early 2018, marking the beginning of a new chapter for hundreds of families.
