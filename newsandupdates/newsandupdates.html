Lead Story · Success

From Lake Victoria Shores to the National Stage

Eight of our youth have been selected to represent Homa Bay County at Kenya's most prestigious youth leadership summit — a testament to nine years of faithful holistic development.

In a remarkable testament to nine years of faithful holistic development, eight of our youth participants have been selected to represent Homa Bay County at the National Compassion Youth Leadership Summit in Nairobi this September. Their journey from shy island children to confident young leaders embodies everything our Centre exists to cultivate — spiritual depth, academic excellence, emotional resilience, and a vision for serving their communities.

"When I first came to the Centre at age seven, I couldn't imagine leaving Mfangano. Now I'm preparing to speak before 500 national leaders about what holistic development looks like from the inside out." Achieng' O., Age 16 · Youth Delegate
