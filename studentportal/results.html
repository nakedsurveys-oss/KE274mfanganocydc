Tutor's Summary

A term of faithful, measurable growth

Grade 6 closed Term II with a class mean of 74.6, a rise of 3.2 points and the fifth consecutive term of improvement. Christian Education remains our flagship (88%), while Computing has been flagged for extra Saturday support from August 15.

Attendance stayed above 95% throughout the term. Learners requiring remedial support have been enrolled in the Goal Achiever catch-up track, in line with our commitment to holistic excellence.
